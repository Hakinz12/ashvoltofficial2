A LITTLE UNIVERSE.

Every great Halloween tale starts with a spark in the dark.

ASHVOLT is a Halloween-inspired creative project built around its pumpkin lead, ASHVOLT, and his friendly ghost companion, Ghostie. The idea brings characters, music, short-form storytelling, and community creativity together.

The goal is simple: make something people want to be part of — and leave room for the community to add its own strange, funny, wonderful ideas.
